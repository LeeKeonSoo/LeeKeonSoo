## Selected Research

### Language-Model Training Data Curation

Developed a traceable framework for language-model training data curation, evaluating data selection through validity, redundancy, quality, and coverage criteria, along with code-model benchmarks.

**My contribution:** Led problem formulation, method design, implementation, experiments, and manuscript writing under my advisor’s guidance.

*First author · “Evidence-Bound Curation: A Traceable Framework for Language-Model Training Data” · Submitted to IEEE Big Data 2026*

### OptimABS

Investigated absolute-value-based adaptive optimization, comparing optimizer variants with RMSProp, Adam, and AdamW on a fixed MLP architecture to examine training-time and accuracy trade-offs.

**My contribution:** Proposed the optimizer idea, led implementation and experiments, and wrote a substantial portion of the manuscript.

*First author and presenter · “OptimABS: Computational Efficiency Improvement of Absolute Value-based Adaptive Optimizer” · KAIC 2025, pp. 35`8–359*

### APPLE

Contributed to research on a low-rank residual path for recovering attention-attenuated evidence.

**My contribution:** Contributed to manuscript writing and ran language-model benchmarks on Pythia-based models incorporating the proposed architecture.

*Second author · “APPLE: Recovering Attention-Attenuated Evidence with a Low-Rank Residual Path” · Submitted to ICLR 2027*
